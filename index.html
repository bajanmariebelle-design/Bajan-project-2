About KCC

An Institution Committed to Learning and Growth

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. is a private educational institution dedicated to shaping well-rounded individuals through quality instruction, meaningful student development, and a supportive learning environment.

Our programs encourage academic excellence, discipline, and personal growth while fostering a community where students, faculty, and families work together toward a shared vision of success.

Mission

Provide quality education that develops students intellectually, morally, and socially.

Vision

To be a leading institution forming competent and compassionate leaders.

Core Values

Integrity, excellence, service, and faith.
